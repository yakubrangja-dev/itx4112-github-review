# 📚 Campus Study Spots Guide

A short guide by students, for students: where to study on and around
campus, depending on whether you need quiet, coffee, or a group table.

> Status: 🚧 In progress — sections are being written by the team.

## Table of Contents
1. Quiet Study Spots
2. Cafés Near Campus
3. Tips for Group Study

---

## 1. Quiet Study Spots
- **Library, top floor**: silent zone, plenty of power outlets
- **Empty classrooms after 4 PM**: free whiteboards, rarely crowded
- **Faculty lounge corner**: small and quiet, good for short sessions

## 2. Cafés Near Campus
- **Corner Café**: quiet in the morning, free Wi-Fi
- **24-hour coffee shop**: good for late-night exam prep

## 3. Tips for Group Study
- **Book a group room**: reserve library rooms a day ahead
- **Use one shared file**: keep all notes in one place so nothing gets lost
